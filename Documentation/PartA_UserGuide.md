# KiddoQuest — User Guide (Part A, iOS)

This guide is for **children (with a grown-up nearby)** and **parents**.

> **Screenshots:** capture them from the simulator (File → Save Screen, or ⌘S) and save them
> in `Documentation/Screenshots/` using the file names shown below, so the images appear here.

---

## For children

### 1. Start

The first time the app opens, Buddy the owl says hello. Tap **Let's Begin!**

![Welcome screen](Screenshots/01-welcome.png)

### 2. Pick an island

The Home screen ("KiddoQuest World") shows four islands. Stars under each island show how much
has been played.

![Home screen](Screenshots/02-home.png)

Tap an island, then tap **Play Island Games**, then tap a game.

### 3. Learn how to play

Every game starts with a picture sentence. It shows what to look at, what to tap, and what happens.
Example for *Feed the Animals*: 🐰 → 👆 → 🥕.

The trail at the top shows where you are: **Learn → Play → Stars**.

Tap the big green **Let's Play!** button.

![Instructions](Screenshots/03-instructions.png)

### 4. Play

- A pointing hand 👆 shows what to tap at the start. If you wait a little, it comes back to help.
- Right answers glow green and Buddy cheers.
- Wrong answers are fine: the picture fades or wiggles gently. Just try again!

![Playing a game](Screenshots/04-playing.png)

### 5. See your stars

When the round is finished you will see:

- **Great Job! 🎉** and Buddy dancing (a bigger dance for 3 stars!),
- your **stars** and how many answers you got right first time,
- a **new sticker** for your sticker book.

Then choose what to do next:

- **🏠 Home** to go back to the islands,
- **🎮 More Games** to pick another game on this island,
- **Play Again** to play the same game again.

![Results](Screenshots/05-results.png)

### 6. Leaving a game early

Tap the round **🏠** button at the top. KiddoQuest asks **Go home?**

- 🏠 **Home** leaves the game.
- ▶️ **Keep playing** goes back to the game.

### 7. My Sticker Book

Tap the ⭐ star counter on the Home screen to open **Rewards**. Your stickers are in
**My Sticker Book**. "?" spaces are stickers still to find.

![Sticker book](Screenshots/06-sticker-book.png)

### 8. AR Learning Mode (real iPhone or iPad)

1. On Home, tap **AR Learning Mode**.
2. Choose *Letter Hunt*, *Shape Hunt* or *Animal Discovery*.
3. Point the camera at a table or the floor and move slowly until the coaching animation finishes.
4. When the pill says **"Tap the table to place the toys!"**, tap the table. Five 3D letters, shapes or animals pop up there.
   (Tap **Move** to put them somewhere else.)
5. Tap any object to explore it: it hops and Buddy tells you something about it.
6. Find the one Buddy asks for ("Find the Triangle!"). After three finds you get stars and a sticker. Tap **Play Again** for new objects.

Always play AR in a safe place with a grown-up.

![AR Learning Mode](Screenshots/07-ar.png)

## For parents

### Opening Parent Settings

Tap the ⚙️ gear on the Home screen and answer the addition question. The sum changes each time,
so children can't memorise it.

![Parent gate](Screenshots/08-parent-gate.png)

### What you can do

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **What [name] learned this week** | Skills from games finished in the last 7 days, with stars, plus this week's games, stars and stickers |
| **Sound Effects** | Soft chimes for right and wrong answers |
| **Background Music** | A calm, quiet melody |
| **Calm Mode** | Turns off confetti and floating animations (also on automatically when iOS *Reduce Motion* is on) |
| **Daily Session Preference** | 15, 20 or 30 minutes a day, or No Limit |
| **Reset All Learning Progress** | Clears stars, stickers, levels and island progress |
| **Recent Learning Sessions** | The last 10 finished games |

![Parent settings](Screenshots/09-parent-settings.png)

### The daily break screen

When today's limit is reached, KiddoQuest shows **Time for a break! 🌙** with a resting owl.
To allow more play today, tap **Grown-ups** and answer the addition question.
Play time resets every new day.

![Break screen](Screenshots/10-break.png)

### Text size and accessibility

- KiddoQuest follows the iOS **Text Size** setting (Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size), within limits that keep the layout child-friendly.
- Dark Mode is supported.
- VoiceOver labels are provided for buttons, cards, stars and game pieces.
